FAQs

Straight Answers, No Guesswork

Pricing, certification, timing, and the air itself, covered below.

How often should a Sarnia home have its ducts cleaned?

Two to three years covers most of the city. Homes closer to the industrial corridor, on gravel shoulders, or with pets and renovation history sit at the short end, and shore properties that close up should fold the cleaning into their spring opening.

Are your technicians certified and insured?

Certified and fully insured on every job, documentation on request. Duct cleaning is not a trade Ontario issues a licence for, so certification, training, and insurance are the credentials with meaning here, and we carry all three.

What evidence do I get of the cleaning?

Camera photos of your trunk lines at the start and the end of the job, reviewed with you on site. In a city whose livelihood is process measurement, we run the same way, nothing on vibes.

How do I know if the dryer vent is the problem, not the dryer?

Heat plus long cycles points at the vent, and so does a hot laundry room or lint at the hood. We clear the full run and measure the airflow, and if the reading says the machine itself is at fault, we say so instead of selling a second visit.

What does the work involve and how long does it take?

Vacuum at the trunks, registers washed, every run brushed under suction, blower compartment cleaned, photos at both ends. Three to four hours typical, longer for larger or older homes with finished basements.

Can you remove odours from the ductwork?

Mostly, in sequence. The cleaning carries out the residue holding the smell, and a sanitizer applied to the clean ducts addresses the remainder. Product sprayed over dust accomplishes nothing, so the order is fixed.

Why does the house dust again within days of cleaning?

Return-side leaks keep feeding the system from the basement, standard for this era of housing. A pressure test locates the leaks, and sealing them is what makes a cleaning stick.

Where do you serve around Sarnia?

The city plus Point Edward, Corunna, Bright's Grove, Camlachie, Mooretown, and down through Petrolia and Forest, with evening slots for shift households.
